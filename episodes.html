Slippery Rails

📍 Brendam Docks → The Main Line → The Old Hill Loop

On a cold, rain-lashed night at Brendam Docks, Henry is tasked with taking the heavy Flying Kipper fish train. When Thomas warns him that wet rails are as slick as glass, Henry arrogantly boasts that "a big, strong engine doesn't need advice from a silly little tank engine" and lies that his sandboxes are full.

Rushing through the gale to escape the smell of fish, Henry misses the points in the fog and is diverted onto the steep, overgrown hill line. On the muddy gradient, his heavy wheels spin violently into useless sparks against the slimy rails. Stranded alone in the pouring rain with the troublesome trucks mocking him in the dark, Henry's pride begins to crumble—until a friendly headlight cuts through the trees...

Featuring: 🟢 Henry 🔵 Thomas 🪵 Troublesome Trucks 👥 Henry's Driver & Fireman 🎛️ The Signalman 🎩 Sir Topham Hatt
