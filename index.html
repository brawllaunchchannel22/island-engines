Slippery Rails

📍 Locations: Brendam Docks • The Main Line • The Old Hill Loop

It is a cold, rain-swept night at Brendam Docks as Henry backs onto a heavy train of fish vans for the Flying Kipper. When Thomas warns him that rain makes the rails as slick as glass, Henry arrogantly boasts that "a big, strong engine doesn't need advice from a silly little tank engine" and lies that his sandboxes are full.

Rushing through the gale to escape the smell of fish, Henry misses the points in the fog and is diverted onto the steep, overgrown hill line. On the muddy gradient, his driving wheels spin violently into useless sparks against the slimy rails. Stranded alone in the pouring rain with the troublesome trucks mocking him in the dark, Henry's pride begins to crumble—until a friendly headlight cuts through the trees...
